2. This might not be very representative of the user because people are complex and can listen to different songs for different reasons.
For example, a person could listen to high energy music when working out, but melodic and wordless music when studying. When averaging these two music
profiles, this could result in an entirely different profile for the user. Maybe instead of creating a single audio aura, it would be more representative to
characterize a few different "moods" from the user in a similar way and showing the percentage of listen time spent within those confines. Another method could
be to group songs within a certain standard deviation of one another in energy, valence, and danceability, and report on the largest group.
